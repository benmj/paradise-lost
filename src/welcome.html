Privacy

Paradise Lost, an illustrated reading guide by Ben Jacobs. Last updated November 23, 2026.

Signing in

  • Reading the guide needs a sign-in, with Google or with a code we email you. It is used to save your reading place and footnotes, to ask the Reader questions, and to see how many people read and how far they get.
  • With Google we receive your email address, name and Google account id, and nothing else; we never see your Google password. With a code we receive only the email address you type, and keep a code for 10 minutes.

What is stored

  • Per reader: email, name, sign-in count, last sign-in, how far you have read in each book, footnotes you save and daily usage counts.
  • Footnotes hold only passages of the public poem, a sentence of each answer and the question you asked.

Ask the Reader

  • Your question and the passage you have open are sent to Anthropic to write the answer. If you bring your own key, it stays in your browser and goes only to Anthropic.
  • Questions are not kept beyond the footnotes you choose to save.

Sharing

  • We do not sell or share your data, and we run no advertising or third-party analytics trackers. YouTube plays through youtube-nocookie.com and loads only when you tap play.

Deleting your data

  • "Delete my data" in the account menu removes your account and everything stored with it. You can also write to the address on benbybenjacobs.com.
