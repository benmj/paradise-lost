# Teaser video

- **Music (Ben's choice, fixed):** Brahms, Intermezzo Op. 118 No. 2, `music/brahms-op118-no2.ogg` (public domain, Musopen via Wikimedia Commons). Never the synthesized score or another track unless Ben asks.
- `record.mjs` records the scenes from a local dev server; `build.py` assembles them with ffmpeg and mixes the music (`TEASER_MUSIC` overrides the path).
- Scratchpad paths in both scripts are from the original session; point `S` at your working folder.
